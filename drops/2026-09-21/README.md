Free ChatGPT companion pack from **Minzy Studios**.

**Nerisca** is an original saltglass otter: slate-teal fur, translucent saltglass ear tips, bioluminescent cyan freckles along the muzzle, calm amber-gold eyes. A quiet tide-pool focus buddy for teens and young adults — not a product mascot, just a giveaway pet you can paste into ChatGPT.

1. Open ChatGPT → new chat (or Custom GPT instructions).
2. Paste everything in `PROMPT.md`.
3. Optional: attach `art/nerisca-portrait.png` as a visual reference.
4. Talk to Nerisca.
